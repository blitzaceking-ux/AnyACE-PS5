Pair a pad

Press Search, then put your pad in pairing mode.

    Nothing pairs by itself: tap Pair on your pad. Your console's DualSense is never paired unless you confirm.

    Before you start: use a second console user
    1. Create a new user on the console and name it after the pad ("PS4", "Xbox"…).
    2. When the console detects the pad and asks which user it is for, choose that new user.
    3. Play with your main user and tap PS to use the pad. Pads without a PS button use Press PS under Players. It does the same.
    4. The console then shows a message about the DualSense taking priority back. To give control back to your pad, press Press PS again.
    5. If the pad stays on the DualSense's user, it works in the console menus but games don't see it.

    Players

    P1–P4 are Bluetooth, P5–P8 wired. The colour is the light bar AnyACE gives that pad.

    Wired controllers

    Xbox One / Series / 360 pads and clones, 2.4G dongles, DualShock 4 / 3, Switch Pro and other HID pads on a USB port are picked up by themselves.
